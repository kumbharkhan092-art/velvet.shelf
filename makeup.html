Common questions

Most setting mists work over any complexion base, but results are best on a fully blended, slightly dried-down look rather than freshly applied wet products.

The formula is fragrance-conscious, but as with any new product, patch testing on your inner arm 24 hours before full-face use is recommended if you have known sensitivities.

Layer thin coats rather than one heavy application, set with mist or powder at the T-zone, and carry the blemish stick for any midday touch-ups rather than reapplying full coverage.
